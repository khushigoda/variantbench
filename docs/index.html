Pipeline steps

  1. Cross-cohort meta-analysis
  2. Heritability & genetic correlation (LDSC)
  3. Independent lead variants
  4. Fine-mapping (SuSiE-RSS) & coding annotation
  5. Colocalization with molecular QTLs
  6. Genome-wide Mendelian randomization
  7. Cis-MR drug-target validation
